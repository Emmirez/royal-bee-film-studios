Save your homepage hero video in this folder with this exact name:

    hero-video.mp4

Optional lighter version for phones:

    hero-video-mobile.mp4
    (then set "On phones" → "lighter mobile video" in Admin → Hero video)

Tips: MP4 (H.264), 1920×1080, 10–30 seconds, no audio needed, ideally under 15 MB.
